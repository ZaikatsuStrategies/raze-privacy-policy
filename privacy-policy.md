# Raze Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

This explains what data Raze ("the Bot") handles.

## Data the Bot stores (in a file on the Bot's host)
- **Server settings:** server ID, log channel ID, welcome channel ID, chat channel ID.
- **Moderation records:** the user's ID, the rule broken, a short reason, severity, the action taken, and the time. Records older than 30 days are deleted automatically. Moderator warnings (`/warn`) are kept until cleared.

## Data the Bot keeps only temporarily
- **Recent messages:** the Bot keeps the last 50 messages per channel (author name, text up to 300 characters) in memory to follow the conversation. This is never written to disk and is lost when the Bot restarts.

## Data sent to a third party
- When someone talks to the Bot, or when auto-moderation checks a message, the message text and recent chat context are sent to an AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a reply or a moderation decision. The provider processes this under its own terms and privacy policy.
- Messages from server staff are not scanned by auto-moderation.

## Other data processed
- **Logs:** when logging is enabled, joins, leaves, deleted and edited messages, and moderation actions are posted to the server's chosen log channel. The Bot keeps no separate copy.
- **Command inputs** such as the user, reason and duration you provide.

## Automated moderation
The Bot may automatically warn, time out or kick members who break the server's rules. Bans are never automatic: a human moderator must approve them. Server admins are responsible for telling their members that automated moderation and logging are in use.

## How the data is used
Only to run chat, moderation, logging and welcome features in the server where it was collected. We don't sell or share it, or use it for advertising or to train AI models.

## Your choices
- `/forgetme` deletes your recent messages from the Bot's memory and your moderation records that are no longer active. Active warnings stay until they expire.
- To request deletion of anything else, contact us below.

## Children
Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in their country). The Bot does not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.
